# Part 2: Homepage FAQ

## FAQ 1

**Q: Is FreelancerReply free?**  
**A:** Yes. FreelancerReply is free while in beta. The free beta allows up to 3 generations per day. Each generation includes Gentle, Firm, and Final Notice versions with email and short DM drafts.

## FAQ 2

**Q: Why is the free beta limited to 3 generations per day?**  
**A:** Each generation creates multiple drafts, and the limit helps prevent automated abuse while keeping the beta free. Three generations gives you up to 9 email versions and 9 short DM drafts per day.

## FAQ 3

**Q: Does FreelancerReply send emails automatically?**  
**A:** No. FreelancerReply only generates drafts. You review, edit, copy, and send the message yourself.

## FAQ 4

**Q: Can I use it for Upwork, Fiverr, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn messages?**  
**A:** You can copy the short DM version and adapt it for platforms where you communicate with clients. Make sure your message follows the platform’s rules and your client relationship context. `[平台规则待确认]`

## FAQ 5

**Q: Is this legal advice?**  
**A:** No. FreelancerReply does not provide legal, financial, accounting, debt collection, or professional advice. Generated emails are drafts only. Review them carefully before sending.

## FAQ 6

**Q: Can I mention late fees or legal action?**  
**A:** Only mention late fees, service suspension, or legal action if you have verified that your contract and applicable rules allow it. FreelancerReply does not verify your legal rights or contract terms.

## FAQ 7

**Q: Do you store my invoice or client details?**  
**A:** The product intent is not to save generator inputs by default. However, inputs may be sent to an AI provider to generate the draft, and technical systems may process logs or rate-limit data. Final implementation and provider details are `[待确认]`.

## FAQ 8

**Q: Is Pro available now?**  
**A:** No. Pro is not available in the current beta. The Pro Solo and Pro Plus plans are planned pricing hypotheses. You can join the waitlist if you want saved clients, brand voice, reminder sequences, or more freelancer email tools.

## FAQ 9

**Q: How much will Pro cost?**  
**A:** Current planned pricing is $9/month or $90/year for Pro Solo, and $19/month or $190/year for Pro Plus. These prices are based on the current pricing calibration report and may be reviewed before payment is launched.

## FAQ 10

**Q: Does Pro include automatic reminders?**  
**A:** Not in the current plan. Automatic reminders, Gmail / Outlook connection, and automatic email sending are not included in P0 and should not be assumed as part of Pro until they are separately validated.

## FAQ 11

**Q: Will there be a lifetime plan?**  
**A:** No lifetime plan is planned for P0 or P1. FreelancerReply has ongoing AI generation costs, so lifetime pricing is not recommended for the early product.

## FAQ 12

**Q: Will this guarantee that my client pays?**  
**A:** No. FreelancerReply helps you draft clearer client communication, but it does not guarantee payment, client response, legal compliance, or any specific outcome.
