Privacy Policy

FreelancerReply is an AI-assisted email drafting tool for freelancers. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

1. Information you enter

When using the generator, you may provide details necessary for drafting your email, such as client names, invoice amounts, project descriptions, or specific circumstances related to late payments. We strongly advise against entering highly sensitive or confidential information unless absolutely necessary for the draft.

2. Waitlist information

If you choose to join the Pro waitlist, we collect your email address. This information is used solely to notify you when new features become available or when the Pro tier launches.

3. Usage and analytics information

We collect anonymous technical data regarding how you interact with the tool. This includes non-identifiable usage patterns, browser type, and device information to help us improve the functionality and user experience of FreelancerReply.

4. AI processing

To generate email drafts, the inputs you provide are sent to an external AI provider via an API. We ensure that our agreements with these providers stipulate that your data is not used to train their foundational AI models.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for analytics, hosting, or waitlist management. These providers only have access to information necessary to perform their functions and are bound by confidentiality agreements.

6. Cookies

FreelancerReply uses essential cookies to ensure basic functionality. We may also use optional analytics cookies to understand usage patterns. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

We retain waitlist email addresses until you opt-out or the service is fully launched. Generator inputs are processed transiently and are not stored in our databases long-term.

8. Your choices

You have the right to request the deletion of your waitlist email address at any time. Because we do not store generator inputs long-term, we cannot retrieve or delete specific generated drafts after your session ends.

9. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@freelancerreply.com.
