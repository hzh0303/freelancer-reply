check_circle Best practices for polite follow-ups

  • task_alt

    Always attach the invoice again. Don't make them search their inbox. Provide the link or PDF in every reminder.

  • task_alt

    Keep it brief. Lengthy emails dilutes the main point: you need to be paid.

  • task_alt

    Assume positive intent first. Invoices often get lost in accounting departments. Start gentle.
